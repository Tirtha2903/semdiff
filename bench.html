Benchmark

Compiles 12 real C functions with your own gcc. Each function is compared with itself at different optimization levels (SemDiff should say "same"), and with two deliberately injected bugs (SemDiff should say "changed"). Needs python3 server.py.

Function-O0 vs -O2-O0 vs -O3Injected bug 1Injected bug 2

How to read it: "same" on the first two columns and "caught" on the last two are correct. "False alarm" means SemDiff reported a change between builds of identical source. "Skipped" means the engine met an instruction it does not support and said so instead of guessing. The corpus is small and written by us, so treat it as a regression suite, not a research claim. Tested on x86-64 with gcc 13.3; vector instructions are disabled in the compile flags.
